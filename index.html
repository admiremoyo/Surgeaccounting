All employers must register with NSSA — whether formal or informal — within 30 days of becoming an employer. NSSA manages two schemes: the Workers Compensation & Insurance Fund (employer-funded) and the National Pension Scheme (funded equally by employer and employee).

To register you'll typically need:

  • Certificate of incorporation
  • CR6 (CR14) and CR5 (CR6)
  • Company contact details (phone, address, email)
  • Certified ID copies of any two directors
  • Business and bank details
  • Employee details (name, ID, DOB, address, salary)

On registration the company receives an SSR number and employees are allocated SSNs. Returns are due by the 10th of each month; late payments attract penalties. We handle the whole process for you.
